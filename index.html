04 / BUILT ON YOUR SAY-SO

Connected businesses.
Independent control.

A useful network should bring work closer, while keeping your decisions and records yours.

01 /

Your approval matters

Quotes, purchases and accounting decisions remain separate approvals. A conversation is not permission to spend your money.

02 /

Your books stay yours

Businesses share selected job evidence, not their entire ledger. One authoritative set of books prevents duplicate postings.

03 /

The record has a reason

Keep the source, the decision and the correction together. Follow a discrepancy back to the evidence that explains it.
