How it works

Three simple steps. Nothing new to learn.

Your munshi, operator or supervisor keeps doing what they already do on WhatsApp. The agent does the rest.

📸
1

Send a photo or voice note

Diary page, hour-meter reading, diesel slip, bill — or just speak in your own language, as you speak it. Send it on WhatsApp like any other message.

🔍
2

AI reads it

AI prepares a draft from the photo or voice note. The workflow asks for a correction or review when needed; a person decides what becomes a record.

📊
3

Owner reviews the draft

The owner reviews the day's draft and handles anything held or unclear. Scheduled summaries and full-day operation still need validation.

🧮 AI reads. Code calculates.For the JCB agent, software rules calculate hours and commission; Gemini reads the submitted number or words.
🗂️ Source and edits stay distinct.The workflows preserve original media and correction history rather than silently replacing the source.
✋ People approve records.Owner review controls confirmation; held or unclear readings are not silently treated as approved.

🧱Live at our kiln

Bhatta Munshi

भट्ठा मुंशी — ईंट भट्ठे की डायरी

A WhatsApp-first kiln diary. The simple version (V6) is running at our own kiln. The munshi sends a page photo, gets a readable Hindi draft, corrects it in the chat and confirms. A new version with a private owner-only audit and accounting is in a controlled pilot.

  • Diary photo → draft in the originating chat
  • Source-bound corrections and version-specific confirmation
  • Private audit in code, so the munshi is never slowed by accounting checks
  • Original source and correction history retained
Explore the kiln workflow

Photo → Hindi draft → munshi corrects and confirms → private audit → owner decision → posted entry. V6 reading and confirmation run daily; in the new pilot, owner approval and automatic posting are still being connected.

V6 live at our own kiln, with a second site coming soon. Accuracy has not yet been formally measured.

🚜Live message tests

JCB Hours Agent

मशीन घंटे का हिसाब — मालिक और ऑपरेटर

A separate construction-machine product with its own AWS stack. Operators send meter photos, diesel slips or Hindi voice notes. The designed flow prepares the previous day's activity for owner review; scheduled delivery and approval in real operation remain unvalidated.

  • Meter-photo routing exercised in builder-run WhatsApp tests
  • Original photos and voice notes kept unchanged
  • Diesel-slip follow-up and held-reading alerts in the workflow
  • Broker report of confirmed hours and commission — test stage
Explore the machine workflow

Operator sends a meter photo or voice note → software checks the reading and continuity → unusual readings are held for owner review → confirmed hours can feed a broker's limited report. The broker flow and a full operator workday have not been validated in live use.

Builder-run message tests only: no full operator workday, recurring 08:00 summary, or real broker use is established; Meta template approval is unconfirmed. Other meter types are synthetic-test scope only.
