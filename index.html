How it works

Three simple steps. Nothing new to learn.

Your munshi, operator or supervisor keeps doing what they already do on WhatsApp. The agent does the rest.

📸
1

Send a photo or voice note

Diary page, hour-meter reading, diesel slip, bill — or just speak in your own language, as you speak it. Send it on WhatsApp like any other message.

🔍
2

AI reads it

The agent reads handwriting, numbers and voice, and asks a simple question if anything is unclear. It does not guess.

📊
3

Owner gets the summary

A clean daily summary on WhatsApp, plus alerts when something looks wrong. You approve — then it becomes the record.

🧮 Code calculates. AI only reads.Totals, hours and balances are worked out by fixed rules in software — not "estimated" by AI.
🗂️ Originals kept unchanged.Every photo and voice note is stored as it was sent. The proof is never edited.
✋ You approve everything.Nothing becomes final, and nothing goes outside, until the owner says OK.
